// how we build

Measured, not guessed

Numbers come from real scans and your own play. When there isn't enough data, the tool says so.

Free, always

Everything tied to the game is free, as Blizzard's add-on policy requires. No paywalls, no premium tier.

Your data stays yours

Only auction house prices are ever shared. Your bags, gold and characters are never sent anywhere without you choosing it.
