How we build

Principles

The same rules for every project.

Measured, not guessed

Numbers come from real data. When there isn't enough yet, the tool says so instead of making something up.

Free where it should be

Everything tied to a game is free, as the game's add-on rules require. No paywalls, no premium tier.

Your data stays yours

Nothing personal leaves your computer unless you choose to sync it, and you can delete it any time.

Support

Like what we make?
