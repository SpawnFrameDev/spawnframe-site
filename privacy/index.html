Privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026

SpawnFrame makes small, free tools. This page explains in plain words what data they use and where it goes. If anything here is unclear, email hello@spawnframe.dev.

// this website

This site has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. It is hosted on Cloudflare, which, like any web host, processes basic request information (such as your IP address) to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

// GoldCraftForever (the addon)

  • Everything is stored on your own computer, in the game's saved-variables file for the addon. SpawnFrame never receives it.
  • Sharing with friends and other players is opt-in and off by default. When you turn it on, only auction house data is shared: item prices, how many are listed, and sales estimates worked out from comparing scans.
  • Never shared: your bags, bank, gold, recipes, characters, farming spots, ledger, alerts or anything else personal.
  • Shared scans travel through the game's own addon messages. Like any in-game message, the receiving player's addon can see the sending character's name. You can choose not to show your name on scans you share.

// GoldCraftForever Companion (coming)

The companion app isn't released yet. When it is, this page will explain exactly what it stores, before you can use it. The plan: market data (prices, listed amounts, sales estimates) is shared once for everyone; anything personal, like your sales history or gold, is stored only if you choose to sign in and sync, is visible only to you, and can be deleted at any time.
