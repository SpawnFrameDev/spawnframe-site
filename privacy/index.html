Privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026

SpawnFrame makes small, free tools. This page explains in plain words what data they use and where it goes. If anything here is unclear, email hello@spawnframe.dev.

this website

This site has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. It is hosted on Cloudflare, which, like any web host, processes basic request information (such as your IP address) to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Tips are handled entirely by PayPal; SpawnFrame only sees what PayPal shows any payee (your name, email and the amount).

GoldCraftForever (the addon)

  • Everything is stored on your own computer, in the game's saved-variables file for the addon. SpawnFrame never receives it.
  • Sharing with friends and other players is opt-in and off by default. When you turn it on, only auction house data is shared: item prices, how many are listed, and sales estimates worked out from comparing scans.
  • Never shared: your bags, bank, gold, recipes, characters, farming spots, ledger, alerts or anything else personal.
  • Shared scans travel through the game's own addon messages. Like any in-game message, the receiving player's addon can see the sending character's name. You can choose not to show your name on scans you share.

GoldCraftForever Companion

The Companion is an optional Windows app. Here is exactly what it does with data.

  • On your PC: it reads the addon's saved-variables file and keeps a copy in a database in your user folder (%APPDATA%\GoldCraftCompanion). It only reads the addon's file; it never changes it. Its pages are served only to your own computer (127.0.0.1).
  • Without signing in, nothing leaves your PC.
  • Signing in is optional and uses your email address and a one-time link. Accounts and data are run by Supabase (servers in the eastern US); the sign-in emails are sent by Resend. SpawnFrame can see your email address and what you uploaded; it is never sold or shared with anyone else.
  • Shared prices (on when you sign in, can be turned off): item prices, how many are listed, sales estimates worked out from comparing scans, and when each scan was made. Every signed-in Companion user can see them. They carry no character names. Everyone's prices are combined: the newest price of each item is kept, and each day's price is the middle value of everyone's uploads that day. Your raw uploads are kept for 2 days while that is worked out; shared history is kept for 45 days. A latest price or sales figure that came from you stays tied to a random account number, so you can delete it.
  • Your own backup (off unless you turn it on): your characters, professions and recipes, ledger, gold and bags. Only you can see it, enforced by the database itself.
  • Checking for updates (can be turned off): about once a day the Companion reads a small file from spawnframe.dev saying what the newest version is. Like visiting any web page, Cloudflare sees that a request was made; nothing in it says who you are. Updates are only installed when you click Update, unless you turn on installing them automatically.
  • Deleting: the Companion's Online page has Delete my online data, which straight away removes everything linked to your account: your raw uploads, your backup, and any latest price or sales figure that came from you. A day's combined price that your upload helped form isn't tied to anyone, so it stays, like a vote that has been counted. To close the sign-in account itself, email hello@spawnframe.dev from the same address. Uninstalling is deleting the app and the folder above.
