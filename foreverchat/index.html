ForeverChat

Sorted chat tabs for WoW Forever. Loot, crafting, group, guild, friends and system messages each get their own tab, and General goes back to being a conversation.

Click a tab. The box at the bottom shows where you'd talk.

The tabs

Every message in its place

Six tabs after General and Combat Log. Each one can be turned off, and its messages go back to General like normal.

GROUP

Party, raid and instance

You're only ever in one group at a time, so they share a tab.

GUILD

Guild and officer chat

Your guild's conversation, without the trade spam in between.

FRIENDS

Whispers from friends

Friends list and Battle.net friends. Whispers from strangers stay in General.

LOOT

Drops and gold

Mob and chest drops, looted gold, auction house sales, and gold in and out at vendors, the mailbox and the auction house.

PROFESSIONS

Crafting and gathering

Your crafts, skill-ups, fishing, mining and herbalism. Other players' "creates…" spam goes here too, or nowhere if you'd rather.

SYSTEM

Game and server messages

Achievements, XP, reputation, errors, friends coming online, and text other addons print.

How it works

Light, and out of the way

The same rules as everything SpawnFrame makes.

Your chat, just tidier

These are the game's own chat tabs, so fonts, colors, timestamps, search, copy and other chat or UI addons keep working.

Click a tab, talk there

Click Group and press Enter to talk to your group, Guild for your guild, Friends to whisper the last friend you talked to. Typing /p or /g still works as normal.

Nothing running in the background

Messages are sorted by type as they arrive. No timers, no scanning, nothing sent anywhere.

In your language

English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil) and Russian. Sorting uses the game's own text, so it works in every client language.

Get started

Install and log in

The tabs appear the first time you log in. Turn them on or off in Options › AddOns › ForeverChat, or type /fchat.
