What it does

Every number comes from your own scans and your own play. When there isn't enough data yet, it says so instead of making something up.

A plan for your day

What to buy, craft and post, in order, with the expected profit. Sized to your gold and to what the market will really take, and it ticks itself off as you go.

What sells, not just what it costs

Sales per day at each house, measured by comparing scans, on every tooltip and behind every recommendation.

Honest profit

Materials you own count at what they'd sell for, so profit means what you gain over just selling them. Farmed materials are free; vials and thread are not.

Professions ranked

Every profession ranked by realistic gold a day, whether you have it or not. Only items that have actually been listed and sold count.

Routes to more gold

Craft and sell, craft and disenchant, buy and disenchant, essence conversions and vendor flips, ranked by gold per hour.

Your auctions, watched

See what you have up, what's been undercut and the price to repost at. Auction mail opens in one click.

Bags that know what things are worth

A Bagnon-style bag window with SELL, HOLD and CRAFT badges, or one click to group everything by what to do with it.

Ledger and gold flow

What actually sold, your earnings per hour, and where every copper went: flights, repairs, training, postage and more.

Prices you can trust

Bait listings don't set the price, old scans are marked, and gear variants like "of the Eagle" get their own price.

Getting started

Five minutes, and it gets better every time you play.

  1. Install it from CurseForge (the app or a download), then log in to WoW Forever.
  2. Type /gcf or click the minimap button. A short tutorial walks you through the window.
  3. Open an auction house. It scans by itself. Scan your faction's house and the Neutral house in Gadgetzan, Booty Bay or Everlook.
  4. Open each profession window once so it knows your recipes and skill, and open your mailbox after sales so the ledger records them.

Sharing, and what stays yours

Friends and other players can share auction house scans, so prices fill in faster.

Only auction house data is ever shared: prices, how many are listed, and sales estimates.

Never shared: your bags, bank, gold, recipes, characters, farming spots or ledger.

Automatic sharing is off until you turn it on. More on the privacy page.

Good to know

  • The game allows one full auction house scan every 15 minutes for your whole account. GoldCraftForever counts down and tells you when the next one is possible.
  • An addon can only read the auction house you're standing at. Friends sharing scans fills in the others.
  • It never buys, posts, cancels or crafts anything on its own. Every action is your click.
